# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last Updated:** June 3, 2026

At Rerendet Farm (operated by Rerendet Coffee Limited), we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and share your personal data when you visit our storefront or make purchases from us, in compliance with the **Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (KDPA)**.

---

## 1. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT
When you interact with our platform, we collect information that can identify you ("Personal Data"). This includes:
- **Registration details**: Your first name, last name, email address, password (stored hashed), date of birth, and gender.
- **Delivery and shipping details**: Your physical address, city, county, town, landmark, and additional address entries.
- **Contact numbers**: Your phone number (stored encrypted using AES-256-CBC).
- **Payment information**: Your Safaricom M-Pesa phone number, and metadata for credit/debit cards (holder name, brand, expiry date, and last four digits). *We do not store your raw CVV or card number.*
- **System telemetry**: IP address, login attempts, user agents, cookies, and device identifiers.

---

## 2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA
We process your Personal Data under the following lawful bases:
- **Performance of a Contract**: To process your orders, arrange delivery with local couriers, manage payments, and issue transaction invoices.
- **Loyalty and Rewards**: To calculate your loyalty points, track your reorder streaks, apply streak bonus multipliers, and credit your store credit wallet.
- **Security & Fraud Prevention**: To monitor login attempts, block IP addresses triggering rate-limit alerts, enforce Two-Factor Authentication (2FA), and maintain activity logs.
- **Consent**: To send you promotional newsletters and marketing campaigns (which you can opt-out of at any time).

---

## 3. HOW WE SHARE YOUR DATA
We do not sell your personal data. We share your information only with trusted third parties to execute storefront functions:
- **Payment Providers**: Safaricom M-Pesa (Daraja API) and card processors (Stripe) to process transaction charges securely.
- **Fulfillment Partners**: Local transport couriers to deliver coffee packages to your designated town and landmark.
- **Infrastructure Providers**: MongoDB Atlas, Upstash Redis, and Vercel for database, caching, and server deployment.
- **Legal Compliance**: When required to do so by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) in Kenya or under statutory legal procedures.

---

## 4. YOUR RIGHTS UNDER THE KENYA DATA PROTECTION ACT (KDPA)
Under the KDPA 2019, you have the following rights regarding your Personal Data:
1.  **Right to be informed**: To know what data we collect, how we use it, and who we share it with.
2.  **Right to access**: To request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
3.  **Right to rectification**: To update or correct inaccurate or incomplete details in your user profile.
4.  **Right to erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")**: To request that we delete your PII. When requested, we anonymize your records to preserve historical sales metrics without identifying you.
5.  **Right to object / Restrict processing**: To request that we halt processing of your data for direct marketing purposes.

---

## 5. SECURITY AND ENCRYPTION
- All connection traffic to our storefront is encrypted using TLS 1.3.
- User telephone numbers and M-Pesa identifiers are encrypted in our databases using AES-256-CBC encryption.
- Account access is protected by rate limits, session timeouts, and option-in Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
