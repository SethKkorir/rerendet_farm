## 3. SHIPPING, DELIVERY, AND BATCHES
3.1. **County Delivery Rates**: Delivery fees are based on your selected delivery zone within Kenya, categorized by county:
- **Nairobi Same-Day**: KSh 150 (Estimated delivery: 1 business day)
- **Kiambu Next-Day**: KSh 200 (Estimated delivery: 1 business day)
- **Mombasa / Kisumu Courier**: KSh 400 (Estimated delivery: 3 business days)
- **Other Regions Courier**: KSh 500 (Estimated delivery: up to 5 business days)
3.2. **Free Shipping**: Orders exceeding KSh 5,000 qualify for free shipping.
3.3. **Product Batches & Freshness**:
- Rerendet Farm processes and roasts coffee in **limited, artisanal batches** to guarantee maximum freshness.
- Each batch is associated with a specific **roast date** and origin.
- Since batches are finite, taste profiles and availability may vary. If a specific batch of coffee beans runs out before your order is fulfilled, we will contact you to offer a refund or an equivalent replacement from the latest roast batch.
